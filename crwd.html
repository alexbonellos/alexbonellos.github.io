CrowdStrike (CRWD): Long

By Alex Bonellos · As of March 2, 2026 · Share price $384.86 · Targets: $392 base, $496 bull

This pitch was built in March 2026, so prices and figures are as of that date. For educational purposes only, not investment advice.

Business overview

CrowdStrike is a leading global cybersecurity company providing cloud-native security to enterprises, governments and critical infrastructure operators. Its AI-powered Falcon platform consolidates endpoint protection, identity security and threat intelligence into a single agent. With over 20,000 customers in 170+ countries, it is the industry standard for stopping sophisticated state-sponsored cyberattacks.

Thesis

1. Iran conflict and prolonged cyber warfare. The market has priced in a short-term conflict bump, but the structural cyber threat will outlast the headlines. Iran's documented threat groups (Charming Kitten, APT33, APT34) target US financial institutions, energy infrastructure and government systems regardless of any ceasefire. Falcon is built to detect exactly these state-sponsored attacks, which repositions CRWD from a growth tech stock into a durable national security asset.

2. AI-powered platform consolidation. CrowdStrike is moving from a pure endpoint vendor to a full AI-native security platform, with Falcon now consolidating 20+ modules. Every AI agent an enterprise deploys creates a new attack surface, which expands CrowdStrike's market. Module adoption reached 49% for 6+ modules and 34% for 7+ (Q3 FY2026), driving wallet share and lower churn. Falcon Flex ARR passed $1.35B, growing 200%+ year on year.

3. Federal spending tailwind. CrowdStrike achieved FedRAMP High authorization in March 2025 across 26 products, opening federal agencies, the defense industrial base and critical infrastructure as customers. Charlotte AI followed in December 2025. Federal contracts are high-margin, multi-year and recurring, a revenue stream the market has not fully priced in.
